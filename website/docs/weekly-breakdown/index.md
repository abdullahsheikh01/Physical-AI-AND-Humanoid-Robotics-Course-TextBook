# Weekly Breakdown

Welcome to the weekly breakdown of the Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics course. This section provides a detailed week-by-week overview of the curriculum, including learning objectives, assignments, and key milestones.

## Course Structure

The course is structured over 13 weeks, with each week focusing on specific topics and hands-on projects. Here's an overview of what you'll learn:

- **Weeks 1-2**: Introduction to Physical AI and foundational concepts
- **Weeks 3-5**: ROS 2 fundamentals and simulation environments
- **Weeks 6-7**: Gazebo physics and collision systems
- **Weeks 8-10**: Isaac Sim and advanced simulation
- **Weeks 11-12**: Perception and navigation systems
- **Week 13**: Capstone project and integration

For detailed information about each week, please see the individual week pages below.
