# Sim-to-Real Transfer

This section covers the concepts and techniques for transferring robotic behaviors and algorithms from simulation to real-world robots, a critical aspect of Module 2: The Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity).

## Simulation vs Reality

The transition from simulation to real-world robotics presents several challenges:

- **Dynamics Differences**: Real-world physics often differ from simulation models
- **Sensor Noise**: Real sensors have noise and imperfections not present in simulation
- **Actuator Limitations**: Real actuators have limitations that simulations may not capture
- **Environmental Factors**: Lighting, surface conditions, and other environmental factors vary

## Transfer Learning Techniques

This section explores various approaches to bridge the sim-to-real gap:

- Domain randomization
- System identification
- Robust control design
- Adaptive algorithms

Learn how to implement sim-to-real transfer techniques with practical examples using Gazebo and Unity simulations.
