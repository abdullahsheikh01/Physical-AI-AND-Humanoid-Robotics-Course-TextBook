## Why Physical AI Matters

Physical AI represents a paradigm shift from traditional digital AI systems to embodied intelligence that operates in the physical world. This approach is crucial for creating robots that can effectively interact with human-centered environments, as they share our physical form and can be trained with abundant data from interacting in human spaces.

### Key Benefits of Physical AI

- **Real-World Interaction**: Unlike digital AI models confined to virtual environments, Physical AI systems operate in the continuous, noisy, and complex physical world
- **Embodied Intelligence**: Physical systems must understand and adapt to the real world, leading to more robust AI
- **Human-Centered Design**: Systems designed to work in human environments create more intuitive and effective interactions
- **Safety and Reliability**: Physical systems must operate safely around humans and property, driving innovation in safe AI
